Set up phone

Paste the webhook URL and key from the Camera quick actions panel in Chief of Staff's chat, then scan the QR code with your phone's camera.

⚠️ The QR code and link contain your key. Don't share them, post them, or leave them on screen. Anyone with them can send to your webhook. Nothing on this page is sent anywhere; the QR is made right here in your browser.

On the phone: scan, open the link, and tap Test send. If you text or email the link to yourself, delete that message afterwards.
